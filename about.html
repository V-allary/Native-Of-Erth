About Us

Native of Erth

Who We Are

Native of Erth is a sustainable streetwear brand built on three decades of experience. We create exclusive, made-to-order pieces from innovative materials like apple, cactus and pineapple leather, organic cotton and hemp. Streetwear with a purpose, made for a movement.

Our Story

Born From the Ground Up

Native of Erth is a streetwear brand built on sustainability, quality, functionality and durability. We blend everyday comfort with purpose, in a style rooted in street culture. With over 30 years of experience in the industry, we've stayed true to one concept from the start: exclusivity. That's why our pieces are made to order, with pre-orders placed before production begins.

We work with apple leather, cactus leather, pineapple leather, organic cotton, hemp and more. Join us, and let's make a movement.

Every collection starts with the same question: would we wear this every day? If the answer isn't yes, it doesn't get made.

The Craft

Made to Be Worn Hard

We care, so we dare. At Native of Erth, we believe real change starts with what others throw away. We give discarded materials a second life, transforming them into considered garments designed to be worn, loved and kept for years.

Relaxed fits, considered details and earthy colours that work with everything you already own.
